A festival with a little more spark

Tradition, with a business twist.

01

Diyaverse is a one-day, learning-focused Diwali fest in our department, blending the warmth of Diwali traditions with the energy of business thinking. Through pitching, marketing, content creation, teamwork and creativity, students get to learn by doing, share ideas and celebrate together.

Open to students of the Department of Business Administration, Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Gorakhpur University.

Choose your challenge

Seven ways to take part.

Each event is judged out of 100 marks unless its scoring format is noted below.

3-minute pitch + 2-minute Q&A per team

Advertisement Challenge

Winning criteria 100 marks

Quiz · Teams of 2

Business Quiz

Slot 1 hourView rules +

3 rounds: Guess the Logo, Guess the Business Person, and Companies, Markets, Finance Terms & Current Affairs

Winning criteria
  • 10 points per correct answer; no negative marking.
  • Highest total score wins; ties are broken in a rapid-fire round.
Rules
  • No phones or books during the quiz.

A good day for everyone

The ground rules.

  • Only Department of Business Administration students can participate; registration is compulsory.
  • Report 15 minutes before your event. Late teams may be disqualified.
  • Respectful behaviour is expected; misconduct leads to disqualification.
  • Students may join multiple events if timings do not clash. Online events can be joined in addition.
  • Judges are faculty and invited seniors; their decision is final.
  • Ties are broken by judges through a short extra task.
